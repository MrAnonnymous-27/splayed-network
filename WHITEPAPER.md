# 📄 SPLAYED ($SPL) Technical Whitepaper (v1.0)

## Executive Summary
SPLAYED ($SPL) is an independent Layer-1 blockchain infrastructure engineered to provide high-throughput, low-latency, and eco-friendly cryptographic transactions. Inspired by Bitcoin's fixed-supply monetary philosophy, SPLAYED enforces strict digital scarcity capped at 21,000,000 $SPL tokens while employing modern consensus algorithms for energy efficiency and fast finality.

---

## 1. Core Economic Architecture & Scarcity

* **Asset Name:** SPLAYED
* **Ticker Symbol:** `$SPL`
* **Maximum Supply:** `21,000,000 $SPL` (Hard-capped)
* **Divisibility:** 8 Decimal Places (1 $SPL = 100,000,000 Splay/Plaid)
* **Inflation Model:** Non-inflationary after full supply emission

The absolute supply limit ensures immunity against inflationary pressure and centralized monetary debasement.

---

## 2. Consensus Mechanism: Eco-PoS / DPoS

SPLAYED transitions away from energy-intensive Proof-of-Work (PoW) mining toward a High-Performance Proof of Stake (PoS) / Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS) architecture.

* **Energy Efficiency:** Reduces network energy consumption by over 99.9%.
* **Validator Network:** Node operators stake $SPL tokens to validate transactions and secure the network.
* **Transaction Finality:** Achieves sub-second to low-second transaction execution.

## 4. Security & Decentralization

* **Cryptographic Standards:** SHA-256 / Ed25519 signature schemes.
